ABOUT

Who Am I?

I'm Aadarsh Soni, an 18-year-old technology enthusiast from Jaipur, Rajasthan, currently pursuing a Bachelor of Technology in Computer Science Engineering.

Ever since childhood, computers, smartphones, and technology fascinated me. I wasn't satisfied with simply using technology—I wanted to understand it. I wanted to know how software is built, how systems communicate, and what happens beneath the surface of every application and device.

That curiosity eventually led me into development.

Today I explore web development, application development, scripting, video editing, graphic design, and everything that helps transform ideas into reality.

But over time I discovered something that fascinated me even more.

Hacking.

To me, development is about creating systems.

Hacking is about understanding them.

Understanding how systems communicate, how vulnerabilities emerge, and how technology behaves at its deepest layers is what ultimately shaped my ambition.

My goal is to become a respected ethical hacker, continuously learning, building, researching, and pushing the limits of my knowledge.

Like many beginners, I was intimidated by coding. I often wondered how anyone could possibly learn so many technologies, languages, and concepts.

What changed everything was realizing that determination grows faster than fear.

Today, I continue learning every day, building projects, expanding my skills, and moving one step closer toward becoming the kind of security professional and technologist I once looked up to.

This journey has only just begun.
